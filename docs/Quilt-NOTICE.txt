PCL Mac contains independently integrated C# adaptations of Quilt semantic
version comparison/range behavior and constraint concepts. Modified for .NET,
resource transactions, bounded solving and remote project selection in 0.14.
Reference: https://github.com/QuiltMC/quilt-loader/tree/0.27.1
The bundled TSV was generated by our own test driver against Quilt Loader 0.27.1.
Quilt Loader itself is downloaded for a selected instance, not bundled in this app.
